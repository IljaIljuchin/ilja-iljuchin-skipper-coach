At sea and on land.

I help founders, executives, and sailing crews make clear decisions under pressure: when the stakes are high, time is short, and everyone is looking to you. Since 2005, I’ve led complex teams and coached leaders through change across organizations including McKinsey & Company, Raiffeisenbank, CETIN, Deloitte, and MallGroup.

Pressure rarely arrives as one big crisis—it builds up when decisions pile up, space to think shrinks, and people rely on you for answers. On an offshore night watch and in a senior leadership role, the dynamic is the same: effort alone stops working, and clarity comes from preparation, honest reflection, and steady action.

My approach is practical, solution-focused, and grounded in real experience. In coaching, that means pairing structured conversation and established tools like HEXACO PI-R, Gallup CliftonStrengths, and Hogan with concrete next steps you can test in your real week. On a boat, it means planning ahead, briefing the crew clearly, setting a sound watch system, and keeping everyone safe and aligned when conditions change.
