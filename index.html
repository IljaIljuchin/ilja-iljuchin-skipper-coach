At sea and on land.

I work with leaders on land and with crews at sea. In both places I help people make clear decisions when a lot depends on them, and keep their energy and their team with them along the way.

I do it because I love the moment when someone stops pushing harder and starts seeing clearly: a leader who finds a way forward they could not see alone, or a crew that comes home closer than it left.

Clients come to me for a calm and honest thinking partner, someone who listens without judging, stays steady when things get heavy and helps them spot options they cannot see from the inside. What they take away is clarity, confidence and a pace they can sustain.
