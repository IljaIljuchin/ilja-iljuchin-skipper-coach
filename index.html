When pressure is high, clarity is what you need most.

Pressure rarely arrives as one big crisis. It builds up: decisions pile up, time to think shrinks, and people look to you for the answer. On a night watch and in a leadership role it works the same way.

The situation

At sea: tired crew, changing weather and a decision that can't wait. In leadership: a full calendar, a team that depends on you for everything, and rest that no longer restores you.

The problem

You can be highly motivated, strong, and work insanely hard… but effort alone stops working. Without clarity and mental resilience you start reacting instead of deciding, and that is where mistakes and exhaustion begin.

How I work with it

See the situation clearly, choose one concrete next step, and agree how to measure progress. On a boat that means a briefing and a watch system. With leaders it means a solution-focused coaching approach with hands-on, experience-based action steps. True resilience comes from mindful self-development.

At sea and on land.

I help founders, executives, and sailing crews make clear decisions under pressure: when the stakes are high, time is short, and everyone is looking to you. Since 2005, I’ve led complex teams and coached leaders through change, including at McKinsey & Company.
