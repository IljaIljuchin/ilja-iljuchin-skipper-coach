Who this is for

You lead people, as a founder, owner, manager or senior leader. You're strong and committed, and you've reached a point where more effort alone no longer works. Maybe you're deciding on a career move, stuck on a plateau, or noticing that your energy drains faster than it recovers.

This is not therapy. It's a structured, practical conversation about how you lead, decide and recover.

What pressure looks like in leadership

The situation

You're delivering, but decisions feel heavier. The team depends on you for every answer, there is little space to think before the next decision, and rest doesn't restore you.

The problem

You keep doing what has always worked: more effort, longer days. At some point that stops working. You start reacting instead of choosing, and the energy you spend is more than the energy you get back.

How I work with it

We slow the situation down, name what actually drives and drains you, and choose one concrete next step. The approach is solution-focused, hands-on and based on experience. Then we check progress against what we agreed.

What you take away

A clear picture of yourself

Your strengths, motivations and blind spots, explored with established assessment tools (HEXACO PI-R, Gallup CliftonStrengths, Hogan).

A concrete next step

For the decision or situation you're facing right now, not a general plan for "someday".

Habits for mental resilience

Practices that fit your real week, built on a solution-focused approach with hands-on, experience-based action steps.

A way to see progress

So you're not guessing whether it's working.

How it starts: a free 30-minute intro session. We talk about what's going on and whether we're a good fit. No obligation.

Questions you might have

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is a structured, practical conversation about how you lead, decide and recover. If it turns out that something else would serve you better, I'll tell you honestly.

What happens in the free intro session?

It's a 30-minute conversation, free and without obligation. You tell me what's going on, and we both see whether coaching is the right next step and whether we're a good fit.

Do we meet in person or online?

Both are possible. I prefer meeting in person, because it brings real benefits: more focus, fewer distractions and a conversation that goes deeper. If distance or your schedule makes that difficult, online works well too.

Where do in-person sessions take place?

Not in a boardroom. We meet in a coaching room, a calm space meant for thinking. The place is chosen together, by agreement.

How are format, schedule and payment arranged?

Individually. We agree on everything after the intro session, based on what you need.

What if my plans change?

It happens, especially with a leader's calendar. Let me know as early as you can and we'll find another time.

Is what we discuss confidential?

Yes. What you share in our sessions stays between us.

How is coaching different from consulting or mentoring?

A consultant or mentor gives you answers from their experience. A coach helps you find your own, and turn them into action. I don't tell you what to do; I help you see your situation more clearly and choose.

In which language do we work?

In English or Czech, whichever suits you better.

Do you also work with teams?

Yes. I have 500+ hours of team coaching and have supported agile transformations in organisations of different sizes. Tell me about your team in the intro call.
