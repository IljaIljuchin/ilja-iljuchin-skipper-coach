Who this is for

You lead people, as a founder, business owner, manager or senior leader. You are capable, committed and used to delivering, yet working harder or longer no longer brings the clarity or energy you need.

Maybe decisions feel heavier, the team looks to you for every answer, or rest has stopped restoring you. Coaching gives you room to step back, see your situation clearly and find a way forward you can sustain. This is not therapy. It is a structured, practical conversation.

Does this sound familiar?

These are the situations leaders bring to coaching most often.

Managing people

“My team needs me, but managing people takes more energy than the work itself.”

Difficult conversations get postponed, the same conflicts keep coming back, and you are never sure how hard to push or when to let go.

For example: a strong performer keeps clashing with the rest of the team, and you have been avoiding the conversation for weeks.

Developing presence and impact

“I know what I want to say, but in the room it doesn't land.”

Your ideas get lost in meetings, someone louder takes the credit, or senior people don't take you as seriously as you would like.

For example: you present to the board, the discussion moves on, and a week later someone else's version of your idea gets approved.

Confidence

“From the outside I look confident. Inside I keep doubting myself.”

A new role, a bigger team or a tough setback, and suddenly you second-guess decisions you used to make easily.

For example: you were promoted six months ago and still feel you have to prove you deserve the job.

Effectiveness of communication

“I explain it clearly, and people still do something different.”

Messages get misread, emails turn into long threads, and you end up repeating yourself or fixing things later.

For example: you agree on priorities in a meeting, and a week later everyone is working on something else.

Being more strategic and understanding the big picture

“I'm so busy with today that I never get to think about where we're going.”

Daily fires eat your week. You know you should step back, but strategy keeps sliding to next month.

For example: the board asks for your three-year view, and you realise you have had no time to build one.

Understanding the self and identity

“I'm good at what I do. I'm just no longer sure it's what I want.”

Success doesn't feel the way it used to. You question your role, your values, or who you are without the job title.

For example: after years of climbing you reach the position you aimed for, and it feels strangely empty.

Dealing with uncertainty and change, decision making

“Everything is changing, and I have to decide without having all the facts.”

Reorganisations, new owners, a market that shifts every quarter. People look to you for answers you don't have yet.

For example: you must choose between two directions for the business, both risky, and the deadline is next week.

Developing others, building teams and delegating

“If I want it done right, I do it myself, and I'm running out of time.”

Letting go is hard, the team waits for your answers, and the people you want to grow don't step up.

For example: you spend evenings fixing work you delegated, while your team leaves at five.

Managing stakeholders and fitting into organisation culture

“Everyone wants something different from me, and I can't satisfy all of them.”

Your boss, the board, peers and your team pull in different directions, and the unwritten rules of the organisation slow you down.

For example: a new CEO changes the priorities, while your team still expects you to protect last year's plan.

Time management, self-care and burnout, work-life balance

“I keep delivering, but rest no longer restores me.”

Long days have become normal, weekends are for catching up, and the people at home get what is left of you.

For example: you notice you are tired on Monday morning, and that it has been like this for months.

What we do

Every situation is different, but we work through it in four steps. We go at your pace and come back to earlier steps whenever we need to.

Where you want to get to

We set a clear goal, build a picture of where you want to be, and connect it with what really matters to you.

Where you are now

We find out where your comfort zone is and work with its edge: what drains you, where your energy is, and what you have already tried.

What is possible

We map your options, the resources and support you already have, and where to seek help, including options you can't see from the inside.

Your next steps

Often small experiments you try in your real week. We look at what worked, adjust, and measure progress together.

Questions you might have

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is a structured, practical conversation about how you lead, decide and recover. If it turns out that something else would serve you better, I'll tell you honestly.

What happens in the free intro session?

It's a 30-minute conversation, free and without obligation. You tell me what's going on, and we both see whether coaching is the right next step and whether we're a good fit.

Do we meet in person or online?

Both are possible. I prefer meeting in person, because it brings real benefits: more focus, fewer distractions and a conversation that goes deeper. If distance or your schedule makes that difficult, online works well too.

Where do in-person sessions take place?

Not in a boardroom. We meet in a coaching room, a calm space meant for thinking. The place is chosen together, by agreement.

How are format, schedule and payment arranged?

Individually. We agree on everything after the intro session, based on what you need.

What if my plans change?

It happens, especially with a leader's calendar. Let me know as early as you can and we'll find another time.

Is what we discuss confidential?

Yes. What you share in our sessions stays between us.

How is coaching different from consulting or mentoring?

A consultant or mentor gives you answers from their experience. A coach helps you find your own, and turn them into action. I don't tell you what to do; I help you see your situation more clearly and choose.

In which language do we work?

In English or Czech, whichever suits you better.

Do you also work with teams?

Yes. I have 500+ hours of team coaching and have supported agile transformations in organisations of different sizes. Tell me about your team in the intro call.
