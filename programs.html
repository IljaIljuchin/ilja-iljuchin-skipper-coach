Who this is for

For leaders and leadership teams who want to practise leadership in real situations, not in a meeting room. On a yacht, how you decide, delegate and handle stress shows within hours, and I help you see it and work with it.

On board I am both your skipper and your coach. As skipper I am responsible for the boat and everyone's safety; as coach I observe how you lead and debrief it with you. One person in both roles means nothing gets lost between the sailing and the learning.

You don't need sailing experience. I take responsibility for the boat and the safety of everyone on board, so you can focus on what you came for.

What you can develop at sea

On a yacht the situations are real: how you decide, delegate and handle stress shows within hours. A week on board, with me as your skipper and coach, is a place to practise and strengthen:

Confidence and self-efficacy

Trust yourself to make the call.

Every day on board brings decisions you can't postpone. Making them, and seeing them work, builds confidence that stays with you.

On board: you plan the next leg, brief the crew and bring the boat safely into a harbour you have never seen.

Leadership, teamwork and communication

Lead a crew, not an org chart.

A small crew in a small space shows quickly how you lead, listen and coordinate, and gives you the chance to practise it.

On board: a sail change at night only works when everyone knows their role and the instructions are clear.

Authority and responsibility

Authority that comes from what you do, not your title.

At sea nobody follows a job title. People follow the person who sets clear expectations, shares the goals and takes responsibility.

On board: you agree on watch roles with people who are senior to you at work.

Work with time and emotions under stress

Stress as an ally, not an enemy.

Pressure at sea is real, and it helps once you know how to work with it: it sharpens your focus and speeds up good decisions.

On board: the wind picks up earlier than forecast, and you decide calmly whether to reef, change course or carry on.

Autonomy and resilience

Keep going when the plan changes.

Weather, fatigue and small breakdowns test how you adapt. You learn to recover quickly and keep a clear head when things don't go to plan.

On board: an engine problem means sailing into port, and the crew looks to you for the new plan.

Self-knowledge and feedback

See how you really lead.

Observation, personality diagnostics and honest debriefs show you how you come across, and what you want to change.

On board: a short debrief every evening on what worked, what didn't and why.

Format

Length

Typically one calendar week, Saturday to Saturday. Shorter formats are possible by agreement.

Who it is for

Leaders, leadership teams and groups of high-potential managers looking for an unconventional way of team building and leadership self-development.

Group size

Typically 8–10 people, depending on the size of the boat (maximum 12).

Dates and destination

By agreement. In some destinations, for example the Adriatic, the boat has to be booked at least six months ahead.

Price

Depends on your requirements: either for the whole voyage for a group of up to 10 people, or per person for mixed groups of leaders from different companies.

How we work together

Before the trip

A consultation about your goals and your team. Where useful, a diagnostic such as HEXACO-PI-R, 4Elements or Sociomapping.

Crew Brief

Passage plan, roles and safety. Everyone goes through the Crew Brief and the Risk Policy before departure.

At sea

Real responsibility every day: planning legs, briefing the crew, leading watches, with observation and coaching on board.

Debrief and back at work

A short debrief every evening and a final one: what you learned and what you take back to your team.

Questions you might have

Do I need sailing experience?

No. I am responsible for the boat and the safety of everyone on board. You learn what you need on the way; the focus is on how you lead, not on sailing skills.

How is this different from a team-building cruise?

A cruise is about the experience. Here every day has a leadership goal: you take real responsibility, I observe how you lead, and we debrief it together.

Is it safe?

Safety comes first. Every programme follows my own Risk Policy and starts with a Crew Brief that every participant agrees to. Read the Risk Policy (PDF).

Can we come as a whole team?

Yes. A leadership team on one boat is one of the fastest ways to see trust, communication and roles as they really are, and to strengthen them.

In which language does the programme run?

English or Czech, depending on the group.

How are dates and price arranged?

Individually. After a consultation about your goals we agree on the format, dates, destination and price.
