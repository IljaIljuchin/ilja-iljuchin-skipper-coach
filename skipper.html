Who this is for

This is for yacht owners, private crews, and teams looking for a dependable offshore skipper on long passages, night watches, and demanding sea legs—whether the goal is a safe passage, building offshore experience, or sailing together without unnecessary stress.

If you want a calm presence on board who prepares thoroughly, communicates clearly, and keeps safety front and centre, we will likely work well together.

Why

Offshore sailing changes quickly: darkness falls, fatigue sets in, and weather or sea state shifts before anyone expects it. When plans are vague or roles are unclear at those moments, small misunderstandings turn into real risk or unnecessary friction.

I skipper because good seamanship is about people as much as boats—giving a crew clear expectations, steady judgment, and confidence so time at sea remains purposeful, safe, and genuinely rewarding.

How

Planning ahead, clear briefings & safety

A skipper who plans ahead, briefs the crew clearly, and protects both boat and people so the trip stays rewarding rather than just an endurance test. Everyone knows the route, safety gear, and expectations before lines come off.

Offshore passages, night watches & 35–46 ft boats

Hands-on experience across non-stop offshore passages, night watches, and 35–46 ft offshore and racing boats. That means steady helm and navigation discipline when the horizon disappears and the passage stretches across days and nights.

Watch systems & calm communication under pressure

Clear watch systems, safety preparation, and calm communication when weather or crew fatigue shifts the plan. Crew members get structured rest and clear updates so decisions stay measured instead of reactive.

Sea miles & dinghy racing sharpness

Informed by ~211 sea days, 2,750+ NM logged, and competitive RS 500 dinghy racing since 2016. Offshore endurance pairs with fast boat-handling feel, sail trim awareness, and close-quarters judgment.

My approach to coaching & how it works

My approach—both on the water and in coaching—is practical, grounded, and solution-focused: clear preparation before pressure builds, honest communication, and concrete next steps rather than drama or guesswork.

How it works: We start with a call to align on dates, boat, route, crew experience, and expectations, then plan the passage, safety briefing, and watch schedule together ahead of departure. (And if you also want leadership or team coaching alongside your work or sailing, we can discuss how that fits too.)

Questions you might have

What kind of passages and boats do you skipper?

I focus on offshore passages, night watches, and sea time on 35–46 ft offshore and racing boats, supported by 2,750+ NM logged, ~211 sea days, and RS 500 dinghy racing since 2016.

How do you handle night watches and changing weather?

Before departure we set clear expectations and a structured watch system so crew members can rest properly. When weather or fatigue shifts, we adjust early with clear briefings and calm communication.

How do we arrange dates, route, and terms?

Individually. Once we connect on a call, we review the boat, destination, timeframe, crew profile, and exact terms based on what the passage requires.

Can I request your full sailing CV and log?

Yes. Reach out and I will share my skippering CV and passage details.
