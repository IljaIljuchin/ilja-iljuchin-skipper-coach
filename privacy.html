Ilja Iljuchin Privacy

Who is responsible

Ing. Ilja Iljuchin, IČO 88162541, Prague, Czech Republic. Questions about your data: privacy@iljuchin.com.

This website

The website itself does not collect or store any personal data and sets no cookies. Visit statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and does not build personal profiles. The hosting provider Vercel keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser) to run the site securely.

Booking a consultation

Bookings run on cal.com. When you book, cal.com processes your name, e-mail address, the time you choose and any note you add, so we can arrange and hold the consultation. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Forms

CV requests and programme enquiries run on Tally, a service based in the EU. Tally processes what you enter in the form (for example name, e-mail, company and message) so I can reply. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

E-mail

If you write to me, I use your e-mail address and message only to reply and to arrange our cooperation.

How long I keep your data

Only as long as needed for our communication and cooperation, and as long as the law requires (for example for accounting). After that I delete it.

Services that process data for me

  • cal.com, bookings: privacy policy (opens in a new tab). Data may be processed outside the EU under the safeguards described there.
  • Tally, forms: privacy policy (opens in a new tab), GDPR (opens in a new tab).
  • Cloudflare, visit statistics: privacy policy (opens in a new tab).
  • Vercel, hosting: privacy notice (opens in a new tab).

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing. Write to privacy@iljuchin.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ (opens in a new tab)).
