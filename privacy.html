Who is responsible

Ing. Ilja Iljuchin, IČO 88162541, Nad Lesíkem 2353/24, 160 00 Prague 6 – Dejvice, Czech Republic. Questions about your data: privacy@iljuchin.com.

This website

The website sets no cookies and does not track you. Visit statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and does not build personal profiles. The hosting provider Vercel keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run the site securely. Legal basis: my legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Booking a consultation

Bookings run on cal.com. When you book, cal.com processes your name, e-mail address, the time you choose and any note you add, so I can arrange and hold the consultation. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Forms

CV requests and programme enquiries run on Tally, a service based in the EU. Tally processes what you enter in the form (for example name, e-mail, company and message) so I can reply. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

E-mail

If you write to me, I use your e-mail address and message only to reply and to arrange our cooperation. Legal basis: steps before or performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), otherwise my legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Coaching

For coaching I need your contact details, session dates and brief notes on your goals and progress. I keep the notes for myself only, in Notion (which is also my CRM) and Google Docs, and share them with no one. I do not record sessions. Online sessions run on Zoom, Google Meet, Apple FaceTime, Jitsi or your company's internal tool, as agreed. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

If you choose to tell me sensitive information, for example about your health, I note it only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time and I will delete those notes.

For corporate programmes paid by your employer, I share only attendance and the number of sessions. What we discuss stays between us unless we agree otherwise.

Sailing and skippering

Before a passage I need details for the crew list: name, date of birth, nationality and travel document number, plus an emergency contact. The crew list is an official document required not by me but by the charter company, marinas and port, customs or border authorities, and in some countries even the coast guard. I pass it only to those who require it. They handle the data as independent controllers under their own rules. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations under the rules of the sailing area (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

If you tell me about a medical condition, allergy or medication, I use it only for your safety on board and delete it after the passage (Art. 9(2)(a) and (c) GDPR).

Invoicing

For every paid service I issue an invoice with your name or company name, address and company ID. I issue invoices through fakturovac.cz. Legal basis: legal obligation under tax and accounting law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Some providers (cal.com, Cloudflare, Vercel, Notion, Google, Zoom, Apple) are based in the US and may process data there. This relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw any consent at any time. Write to privacy@iljuchin.com and I will reply within 30 days. I may verify your identity before acting on a request.

There is no automated decision-making or profiling. Providing data is voluntary, but without it I cannot arrange or provide the service.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ (opens in a new tab)).
